Data and sample documentation

Available source outputs: Descriptive statistics, FEMA sample and results, and Flood Factor sample and results.

Sample-flow table proposed: Provide a sample-flow table for each design. Show transactions and unique PINs before and after every exclusion, followed by treated properties, treated sales, comparison properties, and repeat sellers observed on both sides of the event. Separate preliminary and effective timing, additions and removals, and map cohorts. Do not equate transactions with independent treated units.

Input documentation table proposed: Document each input’s provider, vintage, retrieval date, geographic unit, join key, exclusions, and access conditions. Include sales, parcel universe, parcel geometries, 2019–2020 building footprints, each NFHL vintage, map-release dates and the fixed Flood Factor score vintage. Describe the threshold of five and stable-SFHA endpoint classification. Verify intermediate status changes and the handling of individual map amendments.

Source files: descriptive_statistics.qmd, q1-text-material.qmd, and q2-text-material.qmd. Public availability of a source page does not establish permission to redistribute all underlying records.

Appendix A FEMA identification diagnostics

Source available; article exhibits pending verification: q1_assumption_tests.qmd; local historical rendered output quarto_supportfiles/q1_assumption_tests.html. The historical output is not linked as a published appendix because it is outside the configured website output directory.

Figure A1 proposed: Two event-study panels: additions against never-designated controls and removals against continuously designated controls. Show log-price coefficients, 95% confidence intervals, the omitted reference period, zero and event-date lines, estimator, clustering and treated-PIN support by event bin. Distinguish preliminary from effective dates.

Table A1 proposed: Joint lead tests with statistic, degrees of freedom and p-value; two-year-early placebo coefficients, standard errors, observations and distinct treated properties. Preserve adverse diagnostics. Reconcile the original figure narrative with the joint-test group labels before importing a graphic or declaring a design validated.

Manuscript correspondence: Appendix A, proposed Figure A1 and Table A1. The main text retains only a short interpretation of the diagnostics.

Appendix B Flood Factor identification diagnostics

Source available; article reconciliation pending: q2-identification-diagnostics.qmd, q2_simplermodel_assumptions_and_diagnostics.qmd, and scripts/q2_identification/. The newer diagnostic report requires a complete manifest and matching input, script and output hashes. Its existence alone does not establish successful replication of the historical estimates.

Table B1 proposed: Pre-release slope tests and the 2017 placebo alongside the actual-event specification. Report the high-risk interaction separately from the SFHA triple interaction; show each model’s sample, outcome scale, fixed effects, clustering and cutoff. Audit whether the post indicator varies within the transition year and whether a common post term is estimable with the stated time effects.

Figure B1 proposed: Pre-release group trends by high/low Flood Factor and region, with the release cutoff and group counts. Verify plotted group labels against source data.

Figure B2 proposed: High-versus-low Flood Factor event study using the final article sample and controls. Display leads and lags, confidence intervals, reference period, support counts and a joint lead test. This is a proposed exhibit, not a completed test.

Distinguish public availability in June 2020 from platform adoption and buyer-level exposure. Retain the pretrend and placebo qualifications in the main text. Manuscript correspondence: Appendix B, proposed Figures B1–B2 and Table B1.

Appendix C Outcome transformations and distributional checks

Source available; captions and samples require audit: q2_assumption_graphs.qmd, q2_assumption_graphs_precomputed.qmd, scripts/q2_assumption_tests/, and q1_assumption_tests.qmd.

Figure C1 proposed: FEMA group trends under levels, natural logs, inverse prices and inverse hyperbolic sine. Use the same observations across transformations or explain exclusions. Identify additions/removals and their appropriate controls. Label axes with the exact transformation; inverse prices reverse ordering.

Figure C2 proposed: Equivalent Flood Factor panels, distinguishing scores below five from scores of at least five. Include regional panels and, separately, stable-SFHA comparisons. Check the existing legend against the plotted grouping: some source descriptions refer to SFHA while headings refer to Flood Factor. Add annual group counts to reveal changes in transaction composition.

Figure C3 and Table C1 proposed: Distributional diagnostic plots and test details for each design: method, sample, grouping, statistic, p-value, tuning choices and code location. Verify the reported values of 0.998 and 1 against saved outputs before labeling the exhibits complete.

Similar-looking transformed trends and large diagnostic p-values do not prove parallel counterfactual trends or override adverse placebo results. These exhibits document sensitivity checks rather than certify causal identification.

FEMA supporting results

Available source outputs: FEMA main results and Nearby-property robustness checks.

Full FEMA results table proposed: Full conventional and extended TWFE results, separated by timing and treatment direction. Include restricted comparison groups, cohort and triad trends, building versus parcel exposure, SFHA versus 1-in-500-year zones, and 300-meter restrictions. Report coefficients in log points, standard errors, observations, distinct treated properties and cohort support. Preserve the strong sensitivity of effective-removal estimates to triad trends.

These broader results support main-text Table 3; they should not be reduced to statistically significant specifications. Source files: q1-text-material.qmd and q1-robustness-nearby-properties-check.qmd.

Flood Factor supporting results

Available source outputs: Main Flood Factor results and Alternative score specifications.

Flood Factor contrasts table proposed: Report the relative high-risk-by-post contrast separately from subgroup post shifts. Show the coefficient combinations and covariance-based standard errors used for each marginal contrast, the high-risk subgroup comparison across SFHA status, and the triple-interaction test. These are different estimands.

Flood Factor heterogeneity table proposed: Full regional, binary, categorical and continuous-score specifications, including omitted or dropped terms and uncertainty. Do not interpret different individual significance levels as a significant difference between groups. Avoid describing all regional results as a uniform causal information effect.

Source code: scripts/q2_dissertation_scripts/ and scripts/q2_FF_variable_checks/. Main-text correspondence: Tables 4a, 4b and 5. Match final model/sample labels before transferring outputs.
